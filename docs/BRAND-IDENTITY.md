# Brand Identity

## Purpose

This document defines the current visual identity direction for OrchFlow before the project adds real logo assets, favicon files, installer branding, desktop packaging visuals, or broader interface polish.

The goal is to keep future visual work original, operationally clear, and aligned with the local-first project lifecycle scope.

## Identity Direction

OrchFlow should use an original `OF` monogram as its preferred logo concept.

The monogram should communicate:

- local project flow
- controlled lifecycle operations
- compact operator tooling
- reviewable, human-supervised automation

The mark should feel like it belongs to a local developer operations tool, not a cloud orchestration platform, container product, or generic AI assistant.

## Monogram Concept

The preferred concept is an `OF` mark where:

- the `O` suggests a local flow, cycle, or loop
- the `F` suggests an operational control path
- small nodes may be used to imply start, stop, restart, inspection, or review points
- the overall shape remains legible at small sizes such as a browser tab, launcher icon, or compact login surface

The mark should be simple enough to work in monochrome and should not depend on complex gradients, tiny labels, or decorative detail to be recognizable.

## Visual Tone

The visual direction should be:

- local-first
- calm
- technical
- practical
- trustworthy
- compact enough for dense operator interfaces

Future visuals should support OrchFlow's current web direction: a focused authentication entry and a dense authenticated operator workspace. Branding should clarify the product without turning operational screens into marketing pages.

## Avoided Motifs

OrchFlow should avoid logo or brand concepts based on:

- Docker, Kubernetes, cloud, container, whale, ship, fleet, or nautical motifs
- direct imitation of existing infrastructure, orchestration, or AI product marks
- generic sparkle, magic, autonomous-agent, or chatbot motifs
- artwork that implies remote orchestration, production deployment, or autonomous lifecycle control

These motifs risk confusing the local-first scope, creating avoidable trademark or copyright concerns, or implying capabilities that are explicit non-goals for the current product stage.

## Usage Guidance

Until real assets are designed and reviewed, text-based `OrchFlow` and simple `OF` treatments may remain in the interface.

When future PRs add logo assets, favicons, installer icons, desktop app icons, or branded UI refinements, they should:

- follow this monogram direction
- keep assets original and repository-owned
- verify legibility at small sizes
- document where the asset is used
- update this document if a reviewed visual decision replaces or narrows the concept

## Current Status

This document is descriptive planning only. It does not introduce a logo file, favicon, package asset, installer asset, color system, or frontend behavior change.
